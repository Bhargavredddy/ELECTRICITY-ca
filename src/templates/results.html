Calculation details

Feature construction

Each record uses 24 hourly values for h0–h23.

  • avg_load = mean(h0 … h23)
  • peak_load = max(h0 … h23)
  • morning_load = mean(h6 … h11)
  • evening_load = mean(h17 … h22)
  • load_variance = variance of all 24 hours
